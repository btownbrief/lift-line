      HOW TO RIDE

      Climb. Shed. Repeat.

      1. Lead any set: a single, pair, triple, or quad of one rank.
      2. Follow with the same number of cards at a strictly higher rank, or pass.
      3. When everyone passes, the pile clears and the last rider to lay leads fresh.
      4. 2s are highest and clear the pile immediately.
      5. First out is First Chair; last out lands on the T-Bar.
      Next round: T-Bar gives First Chair their best card. First Chair returns any one card.

      First Chair scores 2. Chairlift scores 1. First to 7 wins the mountain.
